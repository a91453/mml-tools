# Lead Role Arbitration / Anti-Overcleaning
Version: 2026-09-10

This document is a normative SKILL extension for `mabinogi-mobile-mml`.
It overrides any older wording that can be read as “Piano top voice must be removed from T1 unless it is proven to be Vocal.”

## Lead-role core rule

Treat **T1 as the Lead Role, not as a Vocal-only track**.

- A Piano/top voice is **not automatically Vocal Melody**.
- Conversely, a top/lead voice **must not be demoted merely because its Vocal identity is uncertain**.
- During sung sections, a reliable Vocal source has priority for identifying the sung lead.
- During Vocal rests or instrumental sections, a source-supported Piano/Guitar/Keyboard/other instrumental lead may legitimately occupy T1.
- INTRO / INTERLUDE / SOLO / OUTRO must be reviewed as potential **Instrumental Lead** windows instead of being forced into Vocal/Inner categories.

## Positive-evidence requirement for T1 demotion

Any `T1 -> T2/T4/T5/T6` move requires **positive evidence** that the event is accompaniment, inner voice, texture, countermelody, or a non-lead duplicate.

The following are **not sufficient by themselves**:

- “it is not proven to be Vocal”;
- “it is the Piano top voice”;
- “another track can still play the note”;
- “the Full6 version still contains the event”;
- “moving it makes the role labels cleaner”;
- “a statistic improves without source/audio role evidence”.

If positive role evidence is missing or conflicting, preserve the event in the **Source-Faithful Lead Baseline** and mark the role as `PENDING/UNRESOLVED` rather than silently demoting it.

## Multi-source role arbitration

Keep the authority of each source separate:

- **Official / accepted MIDI**: exact pitch, onset, duration, expanded repeat order, exact event identity.
- **Official score / notation**: staff/voice placement, lyrics alignment, written voicing, dynamics, accents, phrasing, D.S./Coda/repeat structure, and lead-vs-inner evidence.
- **Original audio**: actual foreground/background role, timbre, prominence, articulation, sustain, and whether a written part is perceptually present.
- **Mabinogi Mobile output**: minimal adaptation only after source reconciliation.

Do not declare one compatible official source “better” and discard the other. Use compatible sources as complementary evidence. If they conflict, record the conflict and arbitrate with original-audio role evidence and source traceability.

## Lead Demotion Gate

Before accepting any T1 demotion, verify and record:

1. exact source staff / voice / track;
2. whether Vocal is active at that moment;
3. whether an official score places the event in a top/lead voice;
4. whether original audio supports foreground or background role;
5. whether demotion creates a false T1 gap, broken sustain, or broken hand-off;
6. whether T1–T3 still provide complete Lead + Core Harmony + Bass;
7. the positive musical reason the destination role is more correct.

If the required evidence is unavailable, the demotion is `PENDING/UNRESOLVED`, not `PASS`.

## Three-chord single-player rule

For a three-track-capable instrument, the first three tracks must remain a complete single-player core:

- T1 = Lead
- T2 = Core Harmony
- T3 = Core Bass
- T4–T6 = enrichment by default

Important lead/support material may move to T4–T6 only when the Lead Demotion Gate passes **and** the Core3 gate is rerun.

Do not force extra source notes into T2 merely to maximize onset coverage if doing so causes role pollution, large register jumps, low/mid dissonant compression, or removes useful multiplayer enrichment.

## Source-Faithful Lead Baseline

Before role cleanup or six-track reduction, save a Source-Faithful Lead Baseline. Every candidate must be diffed against it and enumerate:

- T1 removed events;
- T1 added events;
- T1-to-other-track moves;
- other-track-to-T1 promotions;
- pitch/onset/duration/volume changes.

A silent role move is forbidden.

## Regression requirement — Rashisa / らしさ

Maintain the historical over-cleaning case as a permanent regression fixture:

- 15 Source Top/Lead events were historically moved `T1 -> T2`;
- the fixture includes beat 259 A4;
- a rule set that demotes those events **only because Vocal identity is uncertain** must warn/fail the Lead Demotion Gate;
- restoring the source-supported Lead events to T1 must preserve the global source-event multiset and continue to pass structural/Core3/Full6 checks.

## Decision order

Use this order:

`source-complete preservation`
→ `Lead identity arbitration`
→ `Core3 completeness`
→ `Mobile minimal adaptation`
→ `T4–T6 enrichment`
→ `theory/statistical cleanup last`

**Role purity must never override source-supported lead continuity.**
